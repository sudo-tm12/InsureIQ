`object-cover`, so exact aspect is forgiving, but keep new assets at this size
so the set stays consistent.

House style for any future cover, so the set reads as one shoot: deep navy wall,
soft natural window light from one side, shallow depth of field, navy ceramic
mug and a small potted plant as recurring props, occasionally a large
semi-transparent pale-blue icon on the wall (shield, checklist). Realistic skin
texture, calm and composed expressions — never distressed, even on difficult
topics like retrenchment or death.

### Casting

The set is cast to reflect South Africa rather than a single group: the earlier
covers were three Black subjects and one pair of hands, and the three above were
added to broaden it across ethnicity, gender and age. Black representation was
deliberately **kept**, not replaced — South Africa is roughly 81% Black African,
so breadth means widening the set, not swapping people out. Keep that balance in
mind when adding covers.

### Prompting note

The generation service's safety filter repeatedly false-positived on explicit
race words and on two-people-together phrasing ("couple", "married", "seated
close together"). Describing appearance visually — skin tone, hair, age — passes
cleanly. Prefer a single subject.
